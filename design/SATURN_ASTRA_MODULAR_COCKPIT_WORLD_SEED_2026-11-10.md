# TO WORLD — Saturn Astra modular cockpit world seed
**Owner design direction — November 10, 2026. Design reference, not a claim of playable implementation or a Meta render order.**

## Core game
Build and iron out ONE reference automobile first: the owner's recognizable black five-door Saturn Astra commuter. The purpose is a reusable car-as-cockpit system that other players can use with this reference module or adapt to a car of their own choosing. Any car, from any manufacturer and era, has the *potential* to be a cockpit module; that is a design goal, not an assertion that every car is already modeled or playable. Player-built finished vehicles may earn a **canon level**; exact criteria, review process, and reward mechanics have not yet been defined by the owner.

The lived-in direction joins automotive tuning and racing culture with a persistent spacefaring society; an automobile remains the player's recognizable cockpit and personal machine. The car's ordinary real-world identity is not inherently poor condition, a joke about neglect, or mandatory aftermarket styling. Render the owner's selected reference faithfully for each shot. A separately selected game/racing version may be tuned or stylized.

## Mechanical docking demonstration — NO morphing
1. A purpose-built, SR-71-inspired airframe is created **without a fixed cockpit**. Its forward cockpit location is an exact missing-car-shaped cavity, not another aircraft cockpit and not a car strapped atop a complete nose.
2. The intact Saturn **drives under its own power through the REAR loading entrance**, as if loading into a vehicle bay.
3. A full-car-width, full-car-height continuous internal drive path/guide track carries the car forward. Its wheels remain supported throughout. Side-cutaway drawings must demonstrate that the drive path reaches the nose cavity without intersecting closed structure.
4. The front wheels seat into a load-bearing cradle and multi-point mechanical locks physically capture the chassis; power/data connectors mate.
5. Black aircraft outer panels partially wrap the car's lower body and rear, while its actual windshield, windows, roofline, antenna, and enough original body remain clearly visible. **The Saturn windshield is the aircraft canopy.** Ryan never gets out of the driver's seat.
6. In the completed aircraft, the recognizable automobile is the structural and functional cockpit module. The airframe fits the car; **the car never morphs, melts, stretches, disappears, or changes shape**.

The owner's two visual design requirements are BOTH binding: (a) show the complete rear-load → interior forward travel → nose socket path, AND (b) show the partially enclosed finished exterior. Do not use one attractive final exterior image as proof of a working route.

## Alternative four-rotor flight attachment
A separate modular flight capability uses **four rotor assemblies** attaching mechanically to the intact car. Once attached, articulated arms **fold outward** to a stable four-rotor lift configuration. The owner's reviewed short film demonstrates the fold-out and takeoff visually; clip-on arrival and actual game physics were not verified in that footage. These are optional separate modules, not permission to replace the SR-71 cockpit mechanism or add rotors to every spacecraft scene.

## Visual DNA / LoRA
For each player's original car, one car-specific LoRA can supply a visual styling guide for modular spacecraft renders: finish, paint, trim, metal texture, silhouette, panel break lines, headlights, glazing and interior character. The GERTECH chassis/functions/ports remain separable. A LoRA-generated image **is not** automatically a working 3D mesh, collision body, or a validated docking joint. First prototype and test the owner's Saturn appearance; permit players to iterate their own.

## Film route / distinction from game
For the owner-selected film redesign, the ordinary Saturn boards a Navy helicopter/amphibious assault carrier through a real vehicle-loading entrance, rides on the **surface** toward the Mariana Trench, then enters the purpose-built cockpitless aircraft from its rear and locks in as the cockpit. The completed craft launches, enters the water, and descends toward the facility. The carrier **does not submerge**. This replaces the earlier concealed Coronado road slab / submersible ferry route for the revised sequence. The older route and original six-paragraph GERTECH MORGUE monologue are history/source material, not to be deleted or paraphrased.

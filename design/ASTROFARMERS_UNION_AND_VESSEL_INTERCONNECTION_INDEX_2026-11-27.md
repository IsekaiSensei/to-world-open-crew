# TO WORLD — ASTROFARMERS UNION, VESSEL ARCHITECTURE & CONNECTED PROJECT INDEX
**Dated design/index record · 27 November 2026**
**Status: Additive design consolidation. Owner-stated concepts are distinguished from open relationships and assistant-proposed interpretations. No game implementation or owner-approved chronology is claimed.**

## 1. Purpose and source boundaries

This page connects the ideas that share a worldbuilding or system-design relationship across the current TO WORLD records. It is an index and integration map, not a replacement World Bible, project objective, game standard, or implementation handoff.

The governing approach is: **connect compatible systems through explicit interfaces; keep independent identities, mechanisms, and projects distinct.** A connection does not make two things synonymous, nor does it authorize merging their code, saves, or canon.

### Provenance labels used here

- **OWNER-STATED:** directly expressed by the owner in the current design conversation.
- **RELATED EXISTING RECORD:** already documented in a linked project/design record.
- **ASSISTANT-PROPOSED / UNCONFIRMED:** a useful interpretation or possible implementation that has not been approved as canon.
- **OPEN / UNRESOLVED:** a relationship or mechanism not yet specified.

## 2. Connected vessel architecture

### 2.1 Dragon — capital-ship / host architecture

**OWNER-STATED CONCEPT:** The Dragon has its own place. The owner described it as somewhat empty, but able to house everything else, initially thinking of it through a capital-ship configuration.

Working interpretation, not a finalized hull specification:
- Treat the Dragon as a **host architecture or vessel-space**, not automatically as the combined identity of every vessel it carries.
- Its interior or modular capacity can house, connect, shelter, transport, or support other systems and vessels.
- The Dragon may assume different configurations according to the needs of a particular game.
- Combining components should add capabilities while preserving their separate identities and usefulness.

**OPEN:** Whether the Dragon physically carries the Behemoth Leviathan, docks it, surrounds it, or represents a separate capital-scale configuration has not been specified. Do not silently decide that relationship.

### 2.2 Behemoth Leviathan — world-colony ship

**OWNER-STATED CONCEPT:** The player begins with a **Behemoth Leviathan**, described as a world-colony ship.

Its defining identity in this record is the colony/world-ship role. It is a starting asset, not another name for the Dragon and not merely a shuttle.

**OPEN:** Internal habitats, population scale, propulsion, colony deployment, and exact relationship to the Dragon remain unspecified.

### 2.3 Fairy Ship — shuttle and nested-scale vessel

**OWNER-STATED CONCEPT:** The starting group also includes a little Fairy Ship. It is small relative to the Behemoth Leviathan, yet giant relative to its own occupants. The owner described it in relation to its own world-colony-ship scale and as a shuttle in the starting arrangement.

Preserve both scales:
- Relative to the Leviathan, it is the small shuttle.
- Relative to its own crew and interior world, it is enormous and can be understood as a world-colony vessel in its own right.

Do not reduce it to a disposable escape pod or assume that its small relative size means it is small to its inhabitants.

**OPEN:** Whether “colony ship” describes its origin, internal world, present function, or all three remains to be clarified. Its independent operating range and docking rules are also open.

### 2.4 Starting arrangement and opening objective

**OWNER-STATED GAME PREMISE:**
- The player starts with a Behemoth Leviathan.
- The player starts with a Fairy Ship.
- The player starts with a crew.
- The opening objective involves **extracting Gertrude from the Fairy Ship**.

This is a starting situation, not yet a complete mission script. The exact condition described as Gertrude being “constipated with a fairy ship” is not normalized here into a specific technical explanation. The extraction is owner-stated; whether Gertrude is trapped, integrated, lodged, entangled, or connected in another way is **OPEN / UNRESOLVED**.

The initial play can therefore begin with a meaningful rescue/recovery operation aboard an already-owned vessel group, rather than requiring the player to build their first ship from nothing. Specific rooms, hazards, controls, and tutorial steps remain unassigned.

### 2.5 Interconnection / “Voltroning” principle

**OWNER-STATED DESIGN DIRECTION:** Everything can interconnect, and the useful configuration depends on what a particular game needs.

The connection principle is broader than one fixed combined robot or capital ship:
- Components and vessels retain their own names, functions, and identity.
- They may dock, carry, host, support, or combine with one another where a defined interface permits.
- A combined configuration can provide capabilities that the parts do not have separately.
- A component must remain useful independently when its role requires independent operation.
- A game's needs determine which configuration is used; no single configuration defines every game.

**DESIGN GUARDRAIL:** “Voltroning” is a combination pattern, not a command to collapse every vessel into the Dragon or to make every component permanently dependent on a larger host.

## 3. Astrofarmers Union — body plans and protection

This section connects the user's robot/farmer design exploration to the wider machine-ecology and vessel setting. It does not claim a finalized species taxonomy or a linear evolutionary timeline.

### 3.1 Environment-shaped bodies

**OWNER-STATED WORLD PRINCIPLE:** The universe shapes moving things through their environment. Body plans should answer the actual environment and task rather than defaulting every machine to a humanoid chassis.

This connects to the deep-time machine-ecology direction already recorded in the [TO DOG World Version 2026-11-20 R2](https://github.com/IsekaiSensei/chat.work.open.ai/blob/main/canon/TO_DOG_WORLD_VERSION_2026-11-20_R2.md), which describes technology becoming integrated into the environment and machine forms adapting to operating conditions.

### 3.2 Turtle and crab working forms

**OWNER-STATED DESIGN DIRECTION:** Astrofarmers' suits and robots may begin with turtle-like and crab-like design language.

- **Turtle direction:** protected central body, shell-like shielding, and a form that can shelter vulnerable components or carried equipment.
- **Crab direction:** segmented structure, articulated limbs, distributed contact/manipulation points, and the ability to grip, brace, climb, dig, or work across irregular surfaces.

These are functional design families, not a claim that every robot is literally one animal or that one must historically evolve into the other. Their exact anatomy, mobility, and task assignments remain open.

### 3.3 Fairy — winged biped form

**OWNER-STATED WORLDVIEW / DESIGN PREMISE:** The winged biped is a meaningful intelligent form in the setting. The owner's statement, “God made man in an Image for a reason,” is retained as the reason this form matters to the design.

Fairy therefore belongs in the index as a significant winged-biped form, not merely as a decorative humanoid skin or an assertion that every machine should use the same body. Specialized turtle, crab, animal-derived, and environmental forms can coexist with it.

**OPEN:** Exact biological, mechanical, spiritual, or technological explanation for the winged-biped form is not specified here.

### 3.4 Directional armor — pyramidal / triangular protection

**OWNER-STATED DESIGN CONCEPT:** A robot intended to withstand projectiles, including advanced projectiles, could use a forward-facing triangular or pyramidal armored form: a pointed frontal approach, a strongly protected face, and a more vulnerable rear.

The inspiration came from noticing faceted/angled forms associated with anti-radar aircraft. Keep the inspiration distinct from the engineering claim:
- Faceting or stealth shaping for radar behavior does **not** by itself establish projectile resistance.
- The triangular/pyramidal frontal geometry is a proposed armor concept, not a verified impenetrable design.
- The deliberately vulnerable rear creates a directional protection problem: orientation, maneuvering, formation, maintenance access, and rear coverage may matter.

Material, angles, thickness, projectile classes, impact behavior, and test results are **OPEN**. Do not claim literal invulnerability.

### 3.5 “A shield” and “shielding” are separate design families

**OWNER-STATED DISTINCTION:** The owner explicitly wants versions of both **a shield** and **shielding**.

Keep the two terms separate:
- **A shield:** a distinct defensive object, structure, or system that can be positioned, carried, deployed, or interposed.
- **Shielding:** protection integrated into or applied across a body, hull, component, or environment.

Either family may later include passive, active, physical, energetic, or mixed implementations if separately designed. Do not assume every shield is a force field, or that shielding is only armor plating. Exact mechanisms and variants remain open.

## 5. Separate project index — Backpack Forge

**OWNER-STATED PROJECT:** **Backpack Forge** is a new project. Its starting question is: the backpack is the default of every RPG character; what makes that character's existence plausible?

It remains its own project rather than becoming a ship subsystem or an Astrofarmers Union branch.

### Explicit connection
- The existing [Universal Game Standards](../GAME_STANDARDS.md) defines the persistent shared backpack as the software authority for portable item ownership and inventory.
- The existing [Universal Item Families design](../design/GERTECH_UNIVERSAL_ITEM_FAMILIES_STONE_BRONZE_FANTASY_2026-11-11.md) defines shared item families and the manufacturing/supply-line direction.
- Backpack Forge can be indexed against those records where its design concerns portable equipment, tools, supplies, crafting, repair, or survival.

### Keep separate
- The universal backpack data system is not the same thing as a physical backpack object.
- The previously documented **Players Backpacks** are an owner-stated future buildable item/equipment family for making worlds ready to host Players. Do not silently rename or replace that concept with Backpack Forge.
- Backpack Forge is not automatically part of the Behemoth, Fairy Ship, Dragon, or Astrofarmers Union.
- A more detailed physical feature list for Backpack Forge is not canonized by this index. Its project-specific design remains to be developed.

## 6. Independent / not-yet-connected index

The following records or ideas have nearby names or themes but must not be merged without an explicit owner-defined relationship:

1. **TO FAIRY World Seed:** its low-poly fantasy realm, constructible 3D rooms, Platonic solids, fabrication, and persistence rules are a separate game/design lane. The shared word “Fairy” does not make the TO FAIRY World Seed the Fairy Ship, the Fairy crew, or the winged-biped body plan.
2. **TO DOG solar Dragon / Dragon power-station imagery:** the November 11 handoff records a solar-winged android riding a Dragon power station. It is related visual/design history, but it does not establish that the power station, the capital-ship Dragon, and any Dragon body are the same entity or configuration.
3. **Universal backpack vs. Players Backpacks vs. Backpack Forge:** software inventory, a manufactured player-entry artifact, and the new Backpack Forge project are three separate indexed concepts.
4. **Dragon vs. Behemoth Leviathan:** the Dragon is the described capital-ship/host architecture; the Leviathan is the described world-colony ship. Their exact physical relationship is open.
5. **A shield vs. shielding:** related defensive ideas, but separate design families.
6. **Orbital travel vs. Gate travel:** connected at the world-system level, but governed by separate mechanisms.
7. **Body-plan forms:** turtle, crab, winged biped/Fairy, and other specialized forms may coexist. No accepted Shell → Crab → Shield → Fairy → Integrated era sequence is established.

- What interfaces allow vessels to connect, combine, separate, and remain independently useful?
- Which Astrofarmers Union tasks favor turtle, crab, winged-biped, or other forms?
- Which shield and shielding variants exist, and what mechanisms do they use?

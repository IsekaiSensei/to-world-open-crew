## 3. Five States of Gold — preserve source terminology carefully

The dossier graphic labels five categories:

1. Crystal — atomic structure in lattice, stable form, data storage.
2. Liquid — flow state, shaping / casting, material transfer, self-repair.
3. Plasma — high energy, transformation, surface fusion, mass processing.
4. Gas — atomic dispersion, coating, infusion, large-scale application.
5. Structural — hardened alloy, construction, load bearing, infrastructure.

**QC qualification:** These are the dossier's graphic/process categories, not five scientifically recognized thermodynamic states of gold. In particular, **Structural** is a construction/material-result category, not a thermodynamic state. Preserve the graphic's intended categories without presenting the title as a formal physics classification.

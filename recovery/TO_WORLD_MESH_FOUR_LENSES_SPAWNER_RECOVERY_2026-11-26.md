## One underlying mesh, four ways to engage with it

The owner describes these as four lenses on the same underlying mesh, **not four products, separate games, or separate realities**:

- **Fantasy** reads the mesh for meaning.
- **Science** reads it for mechanism.
- **Manufacturing** reads it for process.
- **Virtual** reads it for play and operation.

The Gate is the owner's example of one object read through all four lenses:
- Fantasy: a magical connection.
- Science: a design vocabulary including torus, helix and coherence.
- Manufacturing: something requiring gathering, fabrication, assembly and repair.
- Virtual: something an operator can actually use.

These lenses must not be confused with the separate four-layer governance architecture: **Orbital / Gate / Workshop / WorldSeed**. The lenses describe ways of engaging with the same mesh; the governance layers assign ownership to distinct mechanisms. They are different classifications with different purposes.

## MAN U FACTS TURING

**Owner-stated wordplay:** MAN U FACTS TURING.

The owner's breakdown is:
- **Man** — the human.
- **You** — the operator.
- **Facts** — the physical reality of the world.
- **Turing** — computation / AI running the simulation.

The owner's explanation: manufacturing is not just making things. The human participates in making the facts of the world through hands, materials, structure and consequences. Computation is joined to fabrication; the human is not merely watching a computer generate a world.

This is owner-provided philosophical / project wordplay. Do not present it as a scientific etymology, a scientific claim about Turing, or a newly approved implementation architecture.

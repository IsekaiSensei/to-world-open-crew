## Governing architecture

TO WORLD maintains four distinguishable layers:

1. **Orbital layer:** celestial mechanics, trajectories, Lagrange regions, halo/Lissajous orbits, invariant manifolds, rendezvous and station-keeping.
2. **Gate layer:** GERTECH pocket-dimension connections and engineered Gate mechanisms.
3. **Workshop layer:** material processing, making, fabrication, assembly, repair and manufacturing receipts.
4. **WorldSeed layer:** persistent spatial structure, entities, IDs, topology, inventory state and save history.

**Each layer governs its own mechanism. Connections between layers do not make mechanisms interchangeable.**

## Two transport systems must not be collapsed

- **Orbital transportation** uses real orbital mechanics and calculated trajectories. It does not create pocket connections.
- **Gate transportation** uses the separate GERTECH Gate system and pocket-dimension connection rules. It is not a low-energy orbit transfer.

Gate-specific terms (superconducting GOLD-line torus, aligned entangled double helix, stable connection vs chaotic non-connection) remain Gate vocabulary. Do not borrow them to explain manifolds or orbital rendezvous.

## Quantum Table — multi-scale hand interface
**Dated conversational clarification · 26 November 2026**  
**Status: DESIGN CONCEPT. Operating function is owner-stated; physical mechanism is UNRESOLVED / OPEN. No implementation is claimed.**

The **Quantum Table is an instrument, not a destination or merely a display/map**. Its defining function is to let the **same real human hand** act across scale, from **NANO to GLOBAL**, while the operator remains at the same physical table in the same room. The scale/resolution at which the hand can act changes; the room and the operator do not have to be replaced by separate miniature, giant or simulated rooms.

This is a change in the hand's working scale, not merely a camera zoom, cursor metaphor, or level-loading transition. The hand is the operator. Do not describe the Table only as a surface displaying tiny objects.

### Gulliver reference: simultaneous nested realms
A recovered owner statement reads:

> “This is a Titan of functioning societies where the user, if they are looking close enough, are witnessing Gulliver's travels in three stages all at once. There is a man piloting a giant woman being ran by a microscopic realm being ran by a quantum realm behind an ether realm.”

**Source attribution:** Owner statement, recovered from the earlier conversation record. Preserve its original wording, including “being ran”; do not silently normalize the dictation. Preserve the simultaneity and nested relationship when using this reference. The five terms often extracted from that statement — **human operator, giant living organism, microscopic realm, quantum realm, ether realm** — are a useful working index to its named elements, **not a replacement for the four governance layers above** and not yet a separate finalized technical taxonomy. Do not flatten these into sequential destinations or assume the ordering supplies a fully specified causal/engineering model.

### Relationship to the Man in the Pocket
The **science-side Quantum Table** is the multi-scale hand instrument described above. The **Man in the Pocket** has a magical counterpart/expression tied to gamer inventory spaces: a bag, backpack, inventory square or equipment slot can provide access to the persistent pocket-dimension service and its workers. The pocket is the reached environment; the Quantum Table/workshop is where received objects are examined or worked on. Keep this magical inventory access distinct from the science-side Table's hand-scaling function. Their relationship is conceptual/worldbuilding correspondence, not proof that their mechanisms are identical.

The four governance layers remain distinct and retain their existing mechanism ownership. The Quantum Table is a shared **interface for working across them at different scales**; it does not merge Orbital transport, Gate transport, WorldSeed structure or Workshop fabrication into one mechanism. In particular, Orbital movement remains governed by orbital mechanics, and Gate access remains governed by the separate GERTECH Gate design.

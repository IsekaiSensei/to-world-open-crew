## Germanium Properties (Recurring Technical Foundation)

Germanium (Ge) is a metalloid in Group 14. Key properties used:

- Appearance: Shiny, silvery-gray, brittle crystalline solid with metallic luster.
- Density: 5.32 g/cm³.
- Melting point: 938.25°C.
- Crystal structure: Diamond cubic lattice (like silicon).
- Semiconductor: Bandgap 0.66 eV (smaller than silicon’s 1.12 eV), enabling freer electron movement.
- Optical: Highly transparent to infrared; photoconductive (generates current under light).
- Chemical: Relatively stable in air, resistant to most acids.

These properties produce a sleek, semi-translucent, faceted, prismatic blade that can glow with a pulsing multicolor (full-spectrum rainbow) shimmer under light (especially neon), potentially channel energy, and act as more than a simple cutting tool.
